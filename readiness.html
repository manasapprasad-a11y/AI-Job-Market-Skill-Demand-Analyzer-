💡 Resume Structural Optimization Tips
  • ★ Lead with Action Verbs: Shift descriptions to start with "Implemented," "Architected," "Optimized," or "Engineered" to project active ownership.
  • ★ Quantify Business Success Metrics: Convert static statements into metrics, e.g., "reduced computation costs by 22% YOY."
  • ★ Section Separation: Keep your technical skills section highly legible with comma-separated plain text lists.
  • ★ Format Hygiene: Avoid tables, nested grids, or background images inside your document to guarantee optimal parsing by automated ATS crawlers.
